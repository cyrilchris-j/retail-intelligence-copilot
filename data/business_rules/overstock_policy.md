# Overstock policy

Overstock is slow inventory, not merely large inventory.

An item is overstocked only when all of the following hold:
- current stock is at least 1.5× target stock
- inventory coverage exceeds 45 days, or there are no recent sales (undefined coverage)
- average daily sales are at or below 1.5 units, so the item is not a fast seller

A high-stock product with strong velocity is working capital in motion, not a glut.

Recommended manager actions: pause replenishment, consider a transfer to a higher-velocity store, or plan a promotion. None of these actions are executed by the system.
