# Store operations guidance

The network has Indian stores only: Chennai Central, Coimbatore, Bengaluru MG Road, Hyderabad Banjara, and Madurai.

Store attention:
- A store is underperforming when month-to-date units are at least 25% below the simple average of stores
- Investigate local stock-outs before assuming demand has collapsed
- Compare like-for-like categories against peer stores

Questions about Europe, the US, or other geographies cannot be answered from this dataset. Do not generalize Indian store results to other regions.
